Privacy policy

Hermes (Ale) is a personal application used only by its owner, Alessandro Nava. It accesses the owner's own Google account data (Gmail read-only, Calendar, Drive read-only and files it creates, contacts read-only) solely to provide personal assistant features to the owner.

No data is sold, shared with third parties for advertising, or used for any purpose other than providing these features to the owner. Data is processed on a private server controlled by the owner and by the AI model provider the owner has chosen.

The use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
